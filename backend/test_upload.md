# 42 Abu Dhabi — Brand Voice Internal Note

This is a fictional internal brand-voice memo used for testing the
brand-doc-ingest agent. It is NOT an official 42 Abu Dhabi document.

## Things we always do

- Speak as peers, not authorities — students are co-creators of the curriculum.
- Use plain English when possible; technical depth is welcome only when it
  earns its place.
- Foreground accessibility: scholarships, no tuition, no prior coding required.

## Things we never do

- We do not use the word "elite."
- We do not lean on tradition or prestige; we lean on capability and outcomes.
- We do not over-promise: cohorts work hard, and not everyone graduates.

## The aesthetic

- Stark black-and-white photography with one accent of teal or warm orange.
- Sans-serif headlines (heavy weight, tight tracking).
- Lots of negative space around hero imagery.
- Avoid stock photography of "happy diverse coders."
